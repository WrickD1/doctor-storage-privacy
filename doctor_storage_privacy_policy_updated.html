Privacy Policy for Doctor Storage

Effective date: 3 August 2026
Last updated: 12 August 2026

Doctor Storage is developed and operated by Karik Darang Software (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how the Doctor Storage Android application accesses, uses, and handles information.

Doctor Storage is an on-device Android storage diagnosis and media-review application. The current release does not require an account and does not send personal files or scan results to the developer.

1. About this policy

Doctor Storage is designed to help you understand storage use, review files, and perform user-approved cleanup actions while keeping storage diagnosis and review information on your device wherever possible.

2. Information accessed on the device

Depending on the permissions granted and features used, Doctor Storage may access or generate locally:

  • Total, used and free storage measurements.
  • Filenames, type, MIME type, folder, size, date added, date modified and duration exposed by Android.
  • Android-provided image and video thumbnails.
  • Photos, videos and audio exposed through Android MediaStore.
  • WhatsApp media exposed through Android shared media storage.
  • Downloads, documents, PDFs and APK installer metadata.
  • App names, package names and declared permissions visible through Android.
  • Permission-grant status.
  • Android content identifiers needed to open selected items or request deletion.

Doctor Storage does not access WhatsApp chat messages.

3. Permissions

  • Photo and image access is used for storage totals, local review galleries, thumbnails and user-selected eligible image deletion.
  • Video access is used for storage totals, galleries, thumbnails and user-selected eligible video deletion.
  • Music and audio access is used for audio totals, audio and voice-note-style review, and user-selected eligible audio deletion.
  • Legacy shared-storage access is used only on supported older Android versions.
  • Permissions can be denied or revoked in Android settings. Features that depend on that access may then be incomplete.

4. How the information is used

Local information is used to calculate category totals, explain storage pressure, separate ordinary phone media from WhatsApp media where Android metadata permits, group and sort media, display local thumbnails and details, show app-permission information, open selected items, and request deletion only after explicit selection and Android confirmation.

  • Doctor Storage does not automatically delete personal files.
  • Eligible images, videos and audio may be deleted through Android’s system confirmation.
  • Documents, PDFs, APK files and generic Downloads items are review-only in the current release.

5. Data collection and sharing

Doctor Storage processes relevant storage-diagnosis information locally on your device. Advertising SDK data is separate from Doctor Storage’s local storage-diagnosis information.

  • Doctor Storage does not upload personal files.
  • Scan results and file metadata are not transmitted to the developer.
  • Doctor Storage uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to display advertising where advertising is enabled.
  • According to Google’s Mobile Ads SDK documentation, the SDK may automatically collect or share IP address, which may be used to estimate general location; user product interactions; diagnostic information; and device or account identifiers, including advertising-related identifiers where available.
  • Google documents these uses for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes.
  • Doctor Storage does not intentionally send personal photos, videos, audio, WhatsApp media, filenames, scan results, or storage-review decisions to Google AdMob for advertising.
  • Doctor Storage has no developer-operated backend, cloud storage, or cloud AI in the current release.

6. Retention and deletion

The developer does not retain personal files or scan results on servers. Temporary scan results, thumbnails and interface state may exist locally. Local app data can be removed through Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

Selected eligible media is passed to Android’s deletion confirmation. Deletion proceeds only after the user approves Android’s confirmation.

7. Third-party and Android system components

Google AdMob is Doctor Storage’s advertising provider and operates under Google’s own privacy terms and data practices.

Doctor Storage uses Google’s User Messaging Platform and Google consent management platform for advertising-consent choices where required. In applicable regions, users may be asked to Consent, Do not consent, or Manage options.

Advertising behavior can vary according to region, consent choice, device settings, and applicable requirements. Declining consent does not necessarily mean that no ads will be shown.

Android system components are used for permissions, thumbnails, opening files and deletion confirmation.

No Firebase Analytics or separate analytics SDK has been intentionally added by this AdMob integration.

Ad privacy choices

Where required, Doctor Storage provides an in-app option to review or change advertising consent choices previously provided to Google’s consent message. You can find this option in Doctor Storage under Settings & privacy.

8. Security

Processing is kept on-device for Doctor Storage’s storage-review functions and permissions are limited to those functions. No software operation is completely risk-free, and errors or platform limitations may still occur.

You remain responsible for reviewing selected items before approving deletion and for maintaining the security of your Android device.

9. Children’s privacy

Doctor Storage does not intentionally upload personal files or scan results from children or other users. Google Mobile Ads may process advertising and device-related data as described in the Data collection and sharing and Third-party sections of this policy.

10. Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when Doctor Storage features or data practices change.
