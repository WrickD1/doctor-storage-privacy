Privacy Policy for Doctor Storage

Effective date: 3 August 2026
Last updated: 3 August 2026

Doctor Storage is developed and operated by Karik Darang Software (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how the Doctor Storage Android application accesses and uses information on your device.

Doctor Storage is designed as an on-device storage diagnosis and media-review tool. The current version does not require an account and does not send your personal files or scan results to us.

1. Information Doctor Storage accesses on your device

Depending on the permissions you grant and the features you use, Doctor Storage may access or generate the following information locally on your device:

  • Total, used, and free device-storage measurements.
  • Media metadata exposed by Android, such as file name, file type, MIME type, folder, size, date added, date modified, and duration.
  • Android-provided thumbnails for photos and videos so that you can review media visually.
  • Photos, videos, and audio items exposed through Android MediaStore.
  • WhatsApp media files that Android exposes through shared media storage. Doctor Storage does not access WhatsApp chat messages.
  • Files exposed through Android Downloads, including documents, PDFs, and APK installer files.
  • App names, package names, and declared permission information that Android makes visible to the app.
  • Permission status, such as whether photo, video, or audio access has been granted.
  • Android content identifiers needed to open a selected item or request deletion of eligible media.

Doctor Storage uses this information only to provide storage diagnosis, grouping, sorting, preview, review, and user-requested cleanup features.

2. Permissions

Doctor Storage may request:

  • Photos and images access to calculate image storage, show review galleries and thumbnails, and allow user-selected eligible image deletion.
  • Videos access to calculate video storage, show review galleries and thumbnails, and allow user-selected eligible video deletion.
  • Music and audio access to calculate audio storage, identify audio and voice-note-style media, and allow user-selected eligible audio deletion.
  • Legacy shared-storage access on Android 12 and below so the same storage-review functions can work on older supported Android versions.

You may deny or revoke these permissions through Android settings. Features that depend on denied permissions may be unavailable or incomplete.

3. How information is used

Information accessed on your device is used to:

  • Calculate storage totals and category sizes.
  • Explain which storage categories are using the most space.
  • Separate ordinary phone media from WhatsApp media where Android metadata permits.
  • Group media by type, size, folder, and date.
  • Display local thumbnails and file details for review.
  • Highlight large, recent, older, or otherwise useful review groups.
  • Show app-permission information made visible by Android.
  • Open selected files through Android.
  • Request deletion of eligible media only after you select the items and approve Android’s system confirmation.

Doctor Storage does not automatically delete personal files.

In the current version, images, videos, and audio may be eligible for Android-confirmed deletion. Documents, PDFs, APK files, and generic Downloads items may be shown for review but are not deleted by Doctor Storage.

4. Data collection and transmission

The current release version of Doctor Storage:

  • Does not upload your photos, videos, audio, documents, or other personal files.
  • Does not transmit storage scan results or file metadata to us.
  • Does not use a developer-operated backend or cloud storage.
  • Does not use analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • Does not use cloud-based artificial intelligence.
  • Does not require an account.
  • Does not sell personal information.
  • Does not share personal information with advertisers or other third parties.

Processing is performed locally on your device using Android system services and Doctor Storage’s on-device code.

5. Data retention and deletion

Because the current version does not transmit personal files or scan results to us, we do not retain that information on our servers.

Temporary scan results, thumbnails, and interface state may exist locally while the app is running. Local app data is controlled by Android and can be removed by clearing Doctor Storage’s app storage or uninstalling the app.

When you request deletion of eligible media, Doctor Storage sends the selected Android content identifiers to Android’s system deletion flow. Android displays a confirmation prompt. The deletion proceeds only after you approve that system prompt.

6. Third-party services

The current release does not include third-party advertising, analytics, cloud storage, online AI, or active in-app purchasing services.

Android system components may be used to request permissions, display thumbnails, open files, or confirm deletion. Those components are governed by your device settings and Google’s applicable terms and privacy practices.

7. Security

Doctor Storage limits processing to the device and requests only the permissions required for its storage-review functions. No method of software operation is completely risk-free, but keeping personal file processing on-device reduces exposure to external transmission.

You remain responsible for reviewing selected items before approving deletion and for maintaining the security of your Android device.

8. Children’s privacy

Doctor Storage does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users because the current release does not transmit personal files or scan results to the developer.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Doctor Storage’s features, permissions, or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

Future versions that add features such as analytics, advertising, online AI, cloud services, accounts, or billing may require different disclosures. This policy will be updated before such data practices are introduced.
